BLACKTHORN MANOR — ENTRANCE HALL

You stand beneath a cracked chandelier in a silent entrance hall.
A terminal glows on a small desk beside the door.

You have arrived, but before you explore, you need to know exactly where you are.

Find your current location.

Try: pwd

Then look around and see what the manor contains.

Try: ls

When you are ready, move out of the entrance hall and explore another room.

Hint: cd changes your current directory.

You can always return here later.
