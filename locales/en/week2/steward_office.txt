BLACKTHORN MANOR — STEWARD'S OFFICE

Morning light falls across a desk covered in repair orders.
The east wing has been cleared for restoration.

Your first task is to create a new room there named:

restored_room

First orient yourself with pwd and ls.
Then move to the east wing.

To create a directory, use:

mkdir restored_room

Afterward, use ls to verify that the filesystem tree changed.

When the room exists, enter it and create an empty inventory file:

touch inventory.txt

Again, verify the result with ls.

Then return to the manor and inspect the workshop.
