BLACKTHORN MANOR — STEWARD'S OFFICE

Morning light falls across a desk covered in repair orders.
A new part of the estate has opened, but the east corridor still needs restoration.

You are in the service wing.

First orient yourself:

pwd
ls

Your first task is elsewhere in the manor:

ground_floor/east_corridor/restoration_zone

Find a route there.

Remember:

.. means one level up.
~ means your home directory.

When you reach the restoration zone, create a new room named:

restored_room

Use:

mkdir restored_room

Verify the change with ls.

Enter the new room and create an empty inventory file:

touch inventory.txt

Verify again.

Then return to the service wing and inspect the workshop.

You may choose a relative path or a path beginning with ~/terminal-explorer/manor/.
