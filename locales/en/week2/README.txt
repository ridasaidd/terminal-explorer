WEEK 2 — RESTORATION OF THE ESTATE

Blackthorn Manor has been explored.
Now it must be repaired.

This week you will change the filesystem itself.

You will create directories and files, copy records, move and rename objects, and remove things that no longer belong.

Before each change, use the Week 2 habit:

OBSERVE → PREDICT → CHANGE → VERIFY

cat

rmdir
